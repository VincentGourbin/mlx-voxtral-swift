Fluxforge Studio turns your Mac into a complete AI creative studio. Generate high-quality images and videos from text, train your own custom models, and manage your creative library, all locally on your Apple Silicon, with no cloud or subscription required. FORGE YOUR IDEA. A full creative workshop for exploring your visual ideas. Every step is saved in a Git-style branch tree, nothing is lost, everything is recoverable. ADVANCED IMAGE GENERATION. Four Flux 2 models to choose from. VIDEO GENERATION. Create videos from text or images using the LTX-2.3 model. ANIMATION. Animate any generated image in one click. CANVAS EXTENSION. Extend your images beyond their borders with outpainting. LoRA ADAPTERS. Import LoRA adapters from HuggingFace in one click. LoRA TRAINING. Create your own custom adapters directly in the app. BACKGROUND REMOVAL. Remove the background from your images in one click. LIBRARY AND EXPORT.
